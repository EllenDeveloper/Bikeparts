## Entity-Relationship-Diagramm (Textform)

```
ACCOUNT (1) ──────< (N) BIKE
BIKE    (1) ──────< (N) BIKEPART
ACCOUNT (1) ──────< (N) CART
CART    (1) ──────< (N) CART_ITEM
BIKEPART (N) ──< (N) CART_ITEM (über bikepartId)
CART    (1) ──────< (N) SEARCH_RESULT
CART_ITEM (1) ────< (N) PRODUCT_OFFER

Das hier kommt später
ACCOUNT (1) ──────< (N) SEARCH_RESULT
SEARCH_RESULT (1) ──< (N) PRODUCT_OFFER
ACCOUNT (1) ──────< (N) FAVORITE_SHOP
ACCOUNT (1) ──────< (N) BLACKLISTED_SHOP
ACCOUNT (1) ──────< (N) SEARCH_PRIORITY
ACCOUNT (Admin) (1) ──< (N) ADMIN_PARTNER_SHOP
```


in cygwin: 
Account erzeugen
curl -X POST http://localhost:8080/api/accounts -H "Content-Type: application/json"  -d '{    "firstName": "Max", "lastName": "Mustermann","email": "max@example.com"}'

Account mit bike erzeugen  (bei @ManyToOne beziehung ist @JsonIgnore gesetzt!)
curl -X POST http://localhost:8080/api/accounts  -H "Content-Type: application/json"  -d '{    "firstName": "Marion", "lastName": "Mayer","email": "marion@example.com","bikes": [{"name": "bike1","type": "MTB"}]}'

Account mit bike und Bikepart erzeugen  (bei @ManyToOne beziehung ist @JsonIgnore gesetzt!)
curl -X POST http://localhost:8080/api/accounts  -H "Content-Type: application/json"  -d '{    "firstName": "Marion", "lastName": "Mayer","email": "marion@example.com","bikes": [{"name": "bike1","type": "MTB", "bikeparts": [{"name": "bikepart1","type": "CHAIN" }] }]}'
